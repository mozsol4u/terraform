Summary: Rename 8 Azure Subscriptions to Align with Approved Naming Conventions

Description:

As part of the Azure subscription governance and standardization initiative, update the names of the 8 Azure subscriptions to align with the approved naming conventions.

Scope of Work:

Review the current names of all 8 Azure subscriptions.
Validate the required subscription names against the approved naming conventions.
Prepare a mapping of current subscription names to the proposed names.
Rename the subscriptions in accordance with the approved naming standards.
Verify that the updated names are reflected correctly in the Azure portal and relevant management tools.
Update the associated documentation and subscription inventory to reflect the new names.

Acceptance Criteria:

All 8 Azure subscriptions are reviewed against the approved naming conventions.
The current and proposed subscription names are documented and validated.
All 8 subscriptions are renamed according to the approved standards.
The updated names are verified in the Azure portal.
Relevant documentation and inventory records are updated.
Any exceptions or naming conflicts are documented and communicated to the appropriate stakeholders.

Deliverable: All 8 Azure subscriptions renamed in accordance with the approved naming conventions, with the subscription inventory and supporting documentation updated accordingly.
